Modifications in HshVisionLab: IR operators converted to ONNX opset 13,
then encrypted in the existing .hshmodel application package format.
Weights were not trained or modified. Conversion script:
HshDetectionEngin.Tools/ModelTools/convert_vehicle_color.py
Reconstruction uses the original XML/BIN and Protect-Models.ps1.
SHA hashes and comparison against a Torch interpreter of the original
IR are recorded in conversion-report.json. This is numerical validation,
not evidence of accuracy on above-license-plate image crops.

Input: float32 NCHW [1,3,72,72], BGR pixel values 0..255.
Preprocessing is in the graph; do not divide by 255 or apply ImageNet normalization.
Output color: [1,7,1,1], already softmax probabilities in order:
White, SilverGray (original gray), Yellow, Red, Green, Blue, Black.
Original model is intended for front-facing vehicle images. Accuracy on
small body crops is unvalidated. Unsupported colors may be confused with
supported classes, even at a high score. Low confidence returns Unknown.
