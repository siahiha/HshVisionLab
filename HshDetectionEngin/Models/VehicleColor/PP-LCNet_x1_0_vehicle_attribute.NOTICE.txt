Modified for this application: exported Paddle inference graph to ONNX
opset 14 with Paddle2ONNX 1.3.1, fixed the dynamic batch to 1, and packaged
in the application's HSHM0001 .hshmodel format. No training or weight changes.
Rebuild: HshDetectionEngin.Tools/ModelTools/convert_pplcnet_vehicle_color.py RAW_DIR
The script checks source hashes and compares ONNX with Paddle 2.6.2 CPU
inference on five random inputs. See the corresponding conversion-report.json.

Input: float32 NCHW [1,3,192,256], RGB, resize width=256 height=192,
pixel/255, mean=[0.485,0.456,0.406], std=[0.229,0.224,0.225].
Output: sigmoid_2.tmp_0 [1,19], independent sigmoid probabilities.
Indices 0..9: Yellow, Orange, Green, SilverGray (original gray), Red,
Blue, White, Gold (original golden), Brown, Black. Indices 10..18 are vehicle
types and are not consumed. No additional softmax or renormalization.
The application uses argmax of the ten colors without a minimum score.
Scores are not calibrated across models. Numerical export verification
does not establish accuracy on this application's camera images.
